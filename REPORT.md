# Audit report

This repository is an independent reproduction and claim audit for
**Beyond Euclidean Summaries: Online Change Point Detection for
Distribution-Valued Data**.

The paper-scale tangent/optimal-transport mechanism and the finite-dimensional
epsilon-isometry algebra are supported under scoped contracts. The literal
empirical-quantile `ARL₀` wording, the source-table `95%` reduction statement,
and the literal FlowCAP metric/source scope are not supported by the pinned
source and released artifacts. The Reddit result remains blocked because the
exact daily numeric streams and baseline outputs are unavailable.

The detailed claim pages remain under [`logbook/`](logbook/), while the
machine-readable disposition is in [`claims.json`](claims.json) and
[`reproduction_verdicts.json`](reproduction_verdicts.json). Branch roles are
documented in [`branch-audit.md`](branch-audit.md).

These conclusions are evidence-bound and independent. They are not an author
review, an official leaderboard result, or a claim that the broader IDD method
fails.
